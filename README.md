# Oasis

Oasis lives in your MacBook's notch and keeps homework, reminders and focus in one calm place. Click Pip and it's all there.

## Download

**[Download Oasis for Mac](https://github.com/okoushiko/oasis/releases/latest/download/Oasis.zip)** · Version 1.0 · Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

## Install

1. Open **Oasis.zip** from your Downloads, then drag **Oasis** into Applications.
2. Open Oasis. Your Mac says it couldn't verify the app, because Oasis isn't from the App Store. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Oasis.
4. Open Oasis again and click **Open**. You only do this once. Pip walks you through the rest.

## What it does

- Canvas homework, synced from your calendar feed, with reminders the evening before (never during school or sleep)
- To-dos with due times and pop-up reminders
- Focus mode that closes distracting tabs, plus Pomodoro, timers and points you trade for rewards
- Music controls, stocks, battery and AirPods, an image converter, and drag-a-file-to-Claude
- School or Work mode

Your data stays on your Mac.
